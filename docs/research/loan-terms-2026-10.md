# Investor loan program terms — published by lenders (pulled 2026-10-19)

Every figure is from the lender's own program page unless noted; source codes at the bottom. "As low as" rates are advertised floors, not typical rates. Visio and CoreVest blocked fetches (403) and aren't included except where flagged. Kiavi doesn't publish minimum FICO or DSCR loan limits. Reserves and STR DSCR minimums are rarely published.

| Term | Typical range | Sources |
