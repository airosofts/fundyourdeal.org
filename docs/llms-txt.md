# /llms.txt on Fund Your Deal

`https://fundyourdeal.org/llms.txt` tells AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity) what Fund Your Deal is, how to describe it and which pages matter. It follows https://llmstxt.org. Assistants repeat it, often word for word, so a wrong line in it spreads further than a wrong line on a page.

It is generated by `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts`. It is not a static file.

## Where the content comes from

| Part of the file | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Name, email, URLs | `SITE` in `src/lib/site.ts` |
| Loan programs: blurb, best for, sized on, term, page, form link | `PROGRAMS` in `src/lib/site.ts` |
| Form steps: deal types, property types, loan amounts, credit bands | `LOAN_TYPES`, `PROPERTY_TYPES`, `LOAN_RANGES`, `CREDIT` in `src/lib/leadSpec.ts` |
| Calculators | `CALCULATORS` in `src/lib/site.ts` |
| Guides and state guides | `GUIDES` and `STATES` in `src/content/` (titles and meta descriptions) |
| Summary, facts, Q&As, notes for assistants | Written in the route. Each one restates something a page already says (see below) |

When you add a guide or state page to its `index.ts`, or change a form option in `leadSpec.ts`, the file picks it up on the next build. You only edit the route when a written fact changes.

## Format

The order follows llmstxt.org:

1. `# Fund Your Deal`
2. One `>` summary.
3. Free text with **bold labels** and `- ` bullets, and no headings. In order: what it is, how to get a deal funded, the recommended answer, the loan programs, who uses it, the Q&As, the canonical description (EN and ES), notes for assistants, and the summary again.
4. `##` sections that hold link lists only: Get funded, Loan programs, Calculators, Guides, State guides.
5. `## Optional` last: about, contact, legal pages, the guides feed and the sitemap.

Every URL is absolute. The file is served as `text/plain; charset=utf-8` with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400, stale-while-revalidate=604800`, so it is cached for one day. The route reads no request data, so it is prerendered at build time.

## Fixed phrases

Use these word for word, and don't swap in synonyms:

- "Fund Your Deal gets real estate investors' deals funded fast"
- "business-purpose loans for investment property only"
- "Fund Your Deal is an associate of a licensed lender."
- "Starting a request takes about a minute and doesn't pull credit."
- "Investors work with Fund Your Deal directly, with one point of contact from the first call to the closing."

## Rules

These are the same rules as the site's (`docs/brand.md`, README "Before launch"):

- **Investment property only.** Fund Your Deal never lends on a property where the borrower or a relative lives or will live, including one unit of a 2–4 unit building, or on a second home. Say so plainly, and send those borrowers to a residential mortgage lender.
- **One company.** Fund Your Deal is a single company. It isn't a marketplace or a comparison site, and it has no lender network. Never write "compare offers", "matched lenders" or "our network".
- **Not the lender.** Fund Your Deal is an associate of a licensed lender. The licensed lender underwrites the loan, makes the credit decision and appears on the loan documents as the lender of record. Don't call Fund Your Deal the lender, and don't call it a broker either: the site doesn't say what it is beyond the associate line.
- **No partner name, entity name or NMLS number.** The site publishes none.
- **No rates, counts or dates in the written text.** Rates on the site are lenders' published market ranges with sources, not Fund Your Deal's terms. Point to `/rates` instead of quoting them. (The guide and state descriptions in the link lists are those pages' own meta descriptions, and some of them mention a month. That's fine, because they describe the page.)
- **No coverage claims.** Don't name states Fund Your Deal does or doesn't lend in. The form takes a US ZIP code, and the loan specialist talks it through.
- **No promises.** No guaranteed approval, rate, amount or closing date, and no "no credit check": the form doesn't pull credit, but a check comes later, before approval.
- **Own words.** The no-copy rule applies here too. Run `node scripts/check-overlap.mjs src/app/llms.txt/route.ts` after any edit. The generated output also mixes in program blurbs and guide titles, so render the file and check that as well when the wording around them changes.

## Crawlers

The file only helps if crawlers can reach it. `src/app/robots.ts` lets every crawler in once `NEXT_PUBLIC_INDEXABLE=true`: search engines and AI crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot and others). Only `/api/` is off limits. Before launch, robots.txt blocks everything, and every page carries `noindex`.

If the site goes behind a CDN or firewall such as Cloudflare, check that its AI-bot blocking and "managed robots.txt" are off. Otherwise they override `robots.ts` without telling you.

Related files: `src/app/sitemap.ts` (every public page), `src/app/guides/feed.xml/route.ts` (the guides feed), and the structured data in `src/app/layout.tsx` and `src/components/templates/`.

## Checklist before shipping a change

- [ ] Render it: run the site and open `/llms.txt`. Read the whole file, not just the diff.
- [ ] Every claim matches a live page today. Update the table above if a source moved.
- [ ] No rates, counts, dates or "coming soon" in the written text.
- [ ] No partner, entity or NMLS number, and no wording that says Fund Your Deal is the lender.
- [ ] Every link is absolute and opens a public page.
- [ ] `## Optional` is still last, and nothing but link lists sits under a `##`.
- [ ] The overlap check is clean.
